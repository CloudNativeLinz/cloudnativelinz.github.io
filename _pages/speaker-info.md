      <h2 id="speaker-requirements"><span aria-hidden="true">📋</span> Required information</h2>
      <p>Once we've agreed on your speaking slot, please provide:</p>
      <ul class="requirements-list">
        <li><strong>Talk details:</strong> Title and short abstract</li>
        <li><strong>Speaker info:</strong> Your name and professional role</li>

  <section class="community-topic speaker-guidance" aria-labelledby="speaker-tips">
    <h2 id="speaker-tips"><span aria-hidden="true">💡</span> Speaker tips</h2>
    <div class="community-grid">
      <div class="tip-item">
        <h3><span aria-hidden="true">🎯</span> Focus your topic</h3>
        <p>Choose a specific aspect of cloud-native technology that you're passionate about and can dive deep into.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="tip-item">
        <h3><span aria-hidden="true">👥</span> Know your audience</h3>
        <p>Our community includes developers, DevOps engineers, architects, and tech leaders at various experience levels.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="tip-item">
        <h3><span aria-hidden="true">🛠️</span> Include practical examples</h3>
        <p>Real-world use cases, demos, and hands-on examples are always well-received by our audience.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="tip-item">
        <h3><span aria-hidden="true">❓</span> Encourage questions</h3>
        <p>Our community loves interactive sessions. Leave time for Q&amp;A and encourage discussion.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="community-topic speaker-guidance" aria-labelledby="hosting-tips">
    <h2 id="hosting-tips"><span aria-hidden="true">🏢</span> How to be a great host</h2>
    <div class="community-grid">
      <div class="tip-item">
        <h3><span aria-hidden="true">🍕</span> Food &amp; drinks</h3>
        <p>Provide snacks, pizza, or light refreshments. Nothing brings a community together like sharing a meal and good conversations.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="tip-item">
        <h3><span aria-hidden="true">🎤</span> Tech setup</h3>
        <p>Ensure reliable WiFi, projector/screen, and power outlets. The organizers always arrive ahead of time so we can test everything runs smoothly.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="tip-item">
        <h3><span aria-hidden="true">🛋️</span> Welcoming space</h3>
        <p>Create a comfortable environment with clear signage, accessible entrance, and enough seating for 30-50 attendees. You can use <a href="https://github.com/CloudNativeLinz/cloudnativelinz.github.io/tree/main/images/press" target="_blank" rel="noopener">our official logo and press assets</a> for any branding materials.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="tip-item">
        <h3><span aria-hidden="true">🅿️</span> Location &amp; parking</h3>
        <p>Provide detailed directions, parking information, and public transport options. Make it easy for everyone to find and reach your venue.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
